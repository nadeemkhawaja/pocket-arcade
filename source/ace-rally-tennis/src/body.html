How to play

Your only job is the swing

  • Your player runs to the ball on their own. You decide when to swing.
  • Swing a touch early to pull the ball across your body, a touch late to push it the other way. Nail the timing for a PERFECT strike with extra pace.
  • Serving: swing once to toss, then swing again at the top of the toss for the fastest serve.
  • Balls heading out are yours to leave. Do not swing and you win the point.

Webcam

  • Stand back so your head and shoulders are visible. Raise a hand above your shoulder to swing.
  • Right hand aims right, left hand aims left, both hands together play a lob.
  • Good, even light in front of you works best. Keep busy backgrounds out of the side boxes.

Keyboard and mouse

  • SPACE or ENTER or a mouse click to swing.
  • Hold ← or → (or A D) while swinging to aim.
  • Hold ↑ for a lob, ↓ for a drop shot. P pauses, M mutes.

Touch

  • Tap SWING. Hold an arrow with your other thumb to aim.
  • LOB and DROP play those shots directly. Tapping the court also swings.

Scoring

  • Points go Love, 15, 30, 40, then game. At 40 all it is Deuce, and you need two points in a row: Advantage, then Game.
  • Sets go to six games, won by two. At six games all a tiebreak to seven points decides it.
  • Two serves per point. Miss both and it is a double fault. A serve that clips the net and lands in is a let and is played again.
