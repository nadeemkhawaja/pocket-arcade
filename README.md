## Dagger Raid

A side-scrolling commando game in the classic arcade style, drawn in sharp vector art at any screen size.

- Four stages: Jungle Outpost, Frozen Rail Line, Desert Refinery and Iron Citadel, each with its own weather and music
- Four bosses: Mauler, Ironclad, Sky Hammer and General Krome
- You play a beret soldier who builds a team of three: each captive you free picks up a rifle, the first taking point ahead of you and the second covering behind
- Every stage fields its own army in its own uniform: rust guerrillas with machetes and dogs in the jungle, winter riflemen, snipers and paratroopers on the rail line, tan flame troopers at the refinery, black-clad guards and ninjas in the citadel
- An air raid siren winds up at the start of a stage, before big enemy waves and when a boss approaches
- Knife combos (stab, slash, thrust), crouch and jump attacks, parries that cut bullets out of the air, critical hits and backstabs
- Ninja movement: dash roll with a dodge window, counter strikes, double jump somersault, air dash, dive stab and wall kick
- Weapons: Katana (reflects shots), Tri-Knife, Shuriken, Grenade, Rocket and Flamethrower
- Enemy ninjas that vanish in smoke, throw shuriken and strike from behind
- Captives to free, crates, explosive barrels and mines
- Chain bonuses, extra lives at 30,000 points and every 70,000 after, continues and a top-5 high score table
- Four levels (Recruit, Soldier, Veteran, Elite) and an Invincible mode for a no-damage playthrough (scores and medals are off)
- 19 medals, stage select for stages you have reached, two unlockable outfits and Restart Stage in the pause menu
- Upgrade cards: pick one of three power-ups before every stage (12 in all), so each run plays differently
- Style rank from D to S that multiplies chain scores, with a camera push-in on critical hits and finishing blows
- A war-zone film score: marching snare cadence, war drums, urgent low strings, horns and a distant choir, with field gunfire, rolling blasts and far-off battle ambience

## Ace Rally Tennis

Bright stadium tennis with full tennis scoring. Your player runs to the ball on their own; you decide when to swing.

- Swing by raising a hand at the webcam, or play with the keyboard, mouse or touch screen
- Singles, Doubles and Practice modes
- Four opponents (Rookie, Pro, Champion and Legend), courts on Hard, Grass and Clay plus an unlockable Royal indoor court, and match lengths from one game to best of three sets
- Power shot: three perfectly timed swings in a row charge a faster shot that cannot miss
- Invincible rules: a point you would lose is replayed instead (wins do not count toward your record or medals)
- 14 medals to earn
- Career: three cups and nine matches against rising opponents, with skill points to spend on Power, Speed and Control

Keyboard: Space, Enter or a click swings. Hold Left or Right while swinging to aim, Up for a lob, Down for a drop shot. P pauses, M mutes.

The webcam mode needs a secure page (HTTPS), which the live site provides. The browser asks for camera permission the first time.

## Dragon Pagoda

A kung fu climb in hand-drawn HD, inspired by 1980s arcade brawlers but with its own characters.

- Five floors: Lantern Hall, Drum Gallery, Iron Forge, Mirror Shrine and Dragon Summit, each with its own hazard
- Five guardians: Crimson Fan, Jade Spear, Iron Ox, Mirror Monk and Shadow Abbot
- Two fighters: Tao (heavy kicks) and Lian (double jump, faster chi)
- Three-hit combos with launching finishers, sweeps, jump attacks and dagger deflects
- Chi meter and the Dragon Wave super; mash to break free from grabs
- Ninja arts: dash with a dodge window, shadow strikes, flips, wall jumps and critical hits
- Weapons to pick up: nunchaku, bo staff and shuriken, plus enemy ninjas on the upper floors
- Four levels (Novice, Disciple, Master, Grandmaster) and an Invincible mode
- 10 medals, a quick start from any floor you have reached and Restart Floor in the pause menu
- Upgrade cards before every floor (8 in all) and a style rank from D to S
- Its own temple sound: plucked strings, bamboo flute, taiko, woodblock and gong in pentatonic modes
- Floor timer, tally bonuses, continues and a top-5 score table

## Redline Coast

An open-road racer drawn the classic arcade way (scaled sprites on a segmented road), inspired by the great 1980s driving cabinets but with its own cars, stages and soundtrack.

- Five stages: Sunset Bay, Mesa Run, Pine Pass, Neon Harbor and Aurora Ridge, each with its own scenery, weather and hazards
- Race the clock: reach the checkpoint and the goal before time runs out; spare seconds become score
- A race on every stage: you start 8th behind a pack led by a named rival (Marlo, Dusty, Frost, Nova, Zenith) and score by finishing place
- Tunnels, jump ramps, boost pads and police chases that you can outrun or dodge
- On a phone held upright the race fills the whole screen; slide your thumb along the steering bar for smooth, analog steering, with brake and nitro on the right
- Rocket start: hit nitro as the lights say GO
- Drift (tap brake while turning) for a boost, nitro, slipstream, and near-miss chains that multiply the score
- Three cars with different speed, acceleration and grip; four levels from Cruise to Legend
- Pick-ups, twelve medals, high scores, and a touch pad with automatic gas on phones
- Keys: arrows steer and accelerate, Z brakes, Space fires nitro, P pauses, M mutes

## Iron Alley

An original two-player street brawler: own characters, gang, districts and soundtrack. Brick tenements with lit windows, drainpipes, grime and graffiti line the streets; fighters walk in a guarded stance with a weighted stride.

- Two heroes: Kade (heavy hands) and Suki (fast feet); player two can join at any time
- Four districts: Neon Market, Freight Yard, Steel Foundry and Viper Tower, each ending in a boss (Tank, Ratchet, Forge, The Warden)
- Classic belt-scroller fighting with weight: jab, jab, hook; side kick and roundhouse; the back elbow; flying kick; a jumping spin kick on a short recharge
- Two quick hits double an enemy over. Punch him again to grab his head: two punches are knees, the third (or a kick) lifts him over your head and slams him down behind you
- Weapons swing on punch; kicks still work with a bat or pipe in your hand
- Punks pin your arms from behind while a partner hits you; mash any button to break free
- Knock weapons loose and take them (bats, pipes, knives), lift and throw oil drums, and throw the gang into pits (but do not fall in yourself)
- Four levels from Easy to Brutal, lives and continues, twelve medals, high scores with initials
- Keys: player one arrows, Z punch, X kick, C jump, V spin kick; player two W A S D, F punch, G kick, H jump, T spin kick; P pauses, M mutes

## Phones, tablets and computers

All five games are built for current Safari on iPhone and iPad, Chrome on Android, and Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox on Windows and Mac. On a phone, hold it sideways for the biggest picture; held upright, Dagger Raid, Dragon Pagoda and Redline Coast zoom in and follow your fighter so the picture stays large. The pages lock the zoom level and re-measure the screen after every rotation, so a stray double tap or pinch should not leave you looking at oversized buttons.

## Project layout

```
index.html            landing page
dagger-raid/          playable Dagger Raid (one self-contained HTML file)
tennis/               playable Ace Rally Tennis (one self-contained HTML file)
dragon-pagoda/        playable Dragon Pagoda (one self-contained HTML file)
redline-coast/        playable Redline Coast (one self-contained HTML file)
iron-alley/           playable Iron Alley (one self-contained HTML file)
assets/               screenshots for the landing page
favicon.svg           site icon
vercel.json           settings used when the repo is deployed on Vercel
source/               readable source for every game and the build scripts
```

Everything is static: no server code, no build step needed to host it. Art, music and sound effects are all generated in code at runtime.

## Run it locally

```
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000.

## Hosting

The live site runs on Vercel at https://pocket-arcade-rouge.vercel.app/ and is connected to this repository, so every push to `main` publishes a new version automatically.

- **Vercel (another copy):** Add New Project, import this repository, keep the framework preset as Other, then Deploy.
- **GitHub Pages:** Settings, Pages, deploy from the `main` branch and the `/ (root)` folder.

## Changing a game

Edit the files under `source/`, then rebuild the playable pages:

```
python3 source/build_site.py
```

The script bundles each game's `src/` files into a single HTML page and copies it into `dagger-raid/`, `tennis/`, `dragon-pagoda/`, `redline-coast/` and `iron-alley/`.

## Pickleball Tour Pro 3D

Play at `/pickleball/`. Championship pickleball in 3D (three.js r128), imported from the
[Pickleball-Tour-Pro](https://github.com/nadeemkhawaja/Pickleball-Tour-Pro) repository and kept as written.

- Doubles or singles against an AI tour rated 3.5 Club, 4.5 Pro or 5.5+ Master
- Four venues: Grand Slam Arena, Sunset City Rooftop, Emerald Central Park and Malibu Beach Club
- Female or male pro, three outfit colourways, a live umpire calling the kitchen and the two-bounce rule
- Optional webcam swing: raise your hand in the green box
- Controls: W A S D or arrows move, Space drive, E or X dink, Q or Z lob, Shift Erne; on a phone, the touch pad and the LOB, DINK and DRIVE buttons

Source: `source/pickleball-tour-pro/src/index.html`. Its build inlines three.js r128
(MIT licence, `source/pickleball-tour-pro/vendor/LICENSE-three.txt`) in place of the CDN link, so the page needs no outside requests.
